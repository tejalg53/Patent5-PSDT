LEAN CANVAS
A Perceptual Synchronization Method for Adaptive Resource Allocation in Distributed Wearable Haptic Systems Using Dynamic Human Perception Thresholds
2
Problem
  • Uniform synchronization of all wearable nodes regardless of body sensitivity
  • Excessive communication & energy consumption from unnecessary synchronization updates
  • Reduced perceptual quality when network delay or clock drift varies across nodes
4
Solution
  • Dynamically determine body-zone-specific perceptual synchronization thresholds
  • Estimate perceived synchronization error from multiple delay components
  • Compute Perceptual Synchronization Margin (PSM)
  • Classify urgency into four operational states
  • Adaptively allocate synchronization resources only where required
5
Unique Value Proposition
"Synchronize only where human perception demands it - not everywhere equally."
  • Allocates synchronization resources based on perceptual sensitivity
  • Improves efficiency while reducing communication overhead & energy use
  • No compromise to perceived haptic quality
High-Level Concept
  • Adaptive perception-aware synchronization controller for distributed wearable haptic networks
